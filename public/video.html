How it works

Start from a still — either a photo you upload or a product image generated in the production studio, where your product stays pixel-locked. Pick an engine, describe the motion, and generate. The same pipeline covers the finishing work: text-to-speech voice-overs, generated sound effects, and upscaling of images and video at ×4 or ×8. One approved clip or render becomes a full multi-format delivery pack in one action.

Bring your own key — what that actually means

Keou does not run video models itself, and does not resell compute. It is the orchestration layer: your provider key lives in your browser's localStorage, rides each request in an X-Provider-Key header, is used for that single provider call, and is never written to the server's disk, database or logs. You can grep the source for that header and follow the whole path.

The trade-off, stated plainly. Because the key exists only inside a request, the server cannot finish a job on your behalf in a pure BYOK setup — your browser drives the polling. Close the tab mid-generation and the job stays pending until you come back; the studio picks it up again automatically. On a self-hosted install you can set a server-side key so jobs complete unattended, at the cost of that guarantee.

Honest limits

  • Cost is per clip, at your provider's rates. Video is more expensive than images. The platform adds nothing, but the provider bill is real — check your provider's pricing before a large batch.
  • The hosted no-account studio watermarks every video and publishes creations to a public community gallery, with a shared queue: 3 generations running at once, 60 waiting, 20 per network address. A free account keeps work private; a self-hosted install has no watermark, no gallery and no shared queue.
  • Engine quality varies by engine and prompt. These are third-party models; Keou routes to them, it does not improve their physics. Generate, review, re-run — that is the workflow.

Open source, end to end

The full studio — video, images, polish, remix, adapt, packs, voice, sound, upscaling, teams, share links — ships in the public repository under MIT. There is no paid tier holding features back. The documentation covers the API (POST /api/video generates a clip from a still) and the self-hosting guide covers Docker and Railway.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI video engines does Keou support?

Grok Imagine, Kling 2.6 and 3.0, Seedance 2, and Veo 3 — reached through your own KIE.AI or Fal.ai API key. Keou orchestrates these engines; it does not run models itself.

What does bring-your-own-key (BYOK) mean here?

Your key stays in your browser and rides each request in a header — never stored on the server. The trade-off: your browser drives job polling, so a closed tab pauses a job until you return.

Is AI product video generation free on Keou?

The platform is free and MIT licensed. You pay your provider per clip at their rates. Anonymous hosted videos are watermarked and public; a self-hosted install has neither limit.
