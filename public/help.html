Getting Started

Everything you need to set up and use your Keou creative platform. Follow the steps below or jump to any section from the sidebar.

Initial Setup

Get your platform running in under 2 minutes.

1
Log in with the admin credentials provided during deployment.
2
Add your API key — go to Settings and paste your KIE.AI or Fal.ai key.
3
Upload & generate — open the Studio, upload a product image, and hit Generate.
No coding required. Everything is configured from the admin panel.

AI Providers

Keou supports two AI providers. Configure one or both from Settings.

KIE.AI

Credit-based pricing. Predictable budgets.
Get your key

Fal.ai

Pay-per-use. Transparent costs per generation.
Get your key

How to configure

  1. Go to Settings (admin only)
  2. Paste your API key in the provider field
  3. Select your Default Provider
  4. All generations will route through the selected provider

Studio

Your visual production workspace. Upload product images and generate professional visuals at scale.

Workflow

1
Upload one or more product images (drag & drop or click)
2
Select format — choose aspect ratio (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16, 3:4)
3
Creative direction (optional) — describe the scene or mood you want
4
Generate — results appear inline. Click to view full-size

Post-processing

Right-click any result to access:

  • Polish — enhance lighting, textures, and color grading
  • Remix — transform with a custom prompt
  • Adapt — convert to a different aspect ratio
  • Video — animate into a product video
  • Upscale — increase resolution up to 4-8x
Upload multiple images at once for batch processing. 10 images complete in under 5 minutes.

Clients & Projects

Organize your work by client. Each client can have multiple campaigns.

  • Client = a project folder (e.g., "Nike NZ")
  • Campaign = a sub-folder (e.g., "Summer 2026")
  • All generations link to the active client and campaign

Select your client in the Studio before generating. Results are automatically organized in the Library.

Library

All your generations are stored here. Browse, filter, download, and manage your assets.

  • Filter by type (image, video, audio), client, or campaign
  • Bulk select — download, share, move, or delete multiple at once
  • Full-size preview — click any thumbnail

Assets are permanently stored and backed up automatically.
