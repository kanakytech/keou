Getting Started

Everything you need to set up and use your Keou creative platform. Follow the steps below or jump to any section from the sidebar.

The free studio

studio.kanaky.xyz is the full studio, free, with no account. You bring your own KIE.AI key: it stays in your browser, rides each request, and KIE bills your key directly. We never see it and never charge you.

1
Paste your KIE.AI key in the bar at the top. No key yet? The link next to it takes you to KIE.AI, where a key takes two minutes.
2
Pick a tool in the row under the greeting. Create, Polish, Video and Upscale are one click away; More opens the full list with a search.
3
Generate. The estimated cost is written on the button itself, so you know before you click. The first time, the studio asks you to accept that everything you make here is public.
4
Keep what you like. Every result lands in the community gallery with its prompt, and the gallery viewer has a save button. Retouches and upscales open a before/after slider.

Good to know

  • Everything you make here is public — watermarked, in the community gallery, with its prompt. Need private work and downloads? Create a free account, or self-host: no watermark, nothing published.
  • Uploads show their progress — a ring on the thumbnail, a bar with the file name and size. A cross removes an uploaded image if you change your mind.
  • Results appear the second they exist. Long generations (video, Topaz) can take one to two minutes on the provider's side.
  • Costs are estimates, in US dollars, billed by KIE.AI to your key. Your balance is visible on your KIE.AI dashboard.
  • The queue is shared. A few generations run at once for everyone; yours shows its position while it waits.

Initial Setup

Get your platform running in under 2 minutes.

1
Log in with the admin credentials provided during deployment.
2
Add your API key — go to Settings and paste your KIE.AI or Fal.ai key.
3
Upload & generate — open the Studio, upload a product image, and hit Generate.
No coding required. Everything is configured from the admin panel.

Studio

Your visual production workspace. Upload product images and generate professional visuals at scale.

Workflow

1
Upload one or more product images (drag & drop or click)
2
Select format — choose aspect ratio (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16, 3:4)
3
Creative direction (optional) — describe the scene or mood you want
4
Generate — results appear inline. Click to view full-size

Post-processing

Right-click any result to access:

  • Polish — enhance lighting, textures, and color grading
  • Remix — transform with a custom prompt
  • Adapt — convert to a different aspect ratio
  • Video — animate into a product video
  • Upscale — sharp enlargement ×2 or ×4 (20,000 px cap on the longest side)
Upload multiple images at once for batch processing. 10 images complete in under 5 minutes.

Clients & Projects

Organize your work by client. Each client can have multiple campaigns.

  • Client = a project folder (e.g., "Nike NZ")
  • Campaign = a sub-folder (e.g., "Summer 2026")
  • All generations link to the active client and campaign

Select your client in the Studio before generating. Results are automatically organized in the Library.

Library

All your generations are stored here. Browse, filter, download, and manage your assets.

  • Filter by type (image, video, audio), client, or campaign
  • Bulk select — download, share, move, or delete multiple at once
  • Full-size preview — click any thumbnail

Assets are permanently stored and backed up automatically.
