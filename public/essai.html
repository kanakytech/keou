Your key, kept safe

What we do with your API key — and above all what we never do with it.

Your key stays in your browser

It is kept locally (localStorage), on your device only. The “Forget my key” button erases it in one click — there is nothing to delete on our side, because we never had it.

Never written to the database, never to the logs

It travels encrypted (TLS) only at generation time, is held in memory for the length of the job, then discarded. It appears neither in our database, nor in our logs, nor in error messages.

A dedicated key, under your control

Create a key dedicated to this use at kie.ai, cap its budget, and revoke it whenever you like. You keep control from end to end.

Auditable code

The entire platform is open source on GitHub — nothing is hidden. Everything this page promises, anyone can verify line by line.

An honest word: no system is perfect. Use a key dedicated to this use, with a low cap — not your production key.
